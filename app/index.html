        About this guide

        Leonida Ledger is an offline guide and tracker for the 55 PlayStation 5 trophies of Grand Theft Auto VI: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver and 46 bronze, 28 of them hidden on PlayStation until earned. It runs entirely from its own files — no network, no account, no tracking. Your checklist, counters, notes and settings live in this browser only (localStorage) and can be exported to a file and imported back.

        How the guides were written

        Grand Theft Auto VI released on 19 November 2026, the day this guide was built, so no guide here has been checked in the game yet. Every guide is written in our own words from the trophy's official description and the counts PlayStation tracks — and where a place or step is not pinned down by the official description, the guide says so instead of guessing. That is the honest trade: a cited guide that admits what it does not know beats a detailed one that invents a location.

        How to use the tracker

        • Tick a trophy when it pops on your PlayStation — it stays ticked in this browser.
        • Counted trophies (stunts, cards, WAiNK spots and the rest) have a − / + counter: log the running total as you play.
        • Open any trophy for its full guide, its sources, its roadmap stage and a place for your own note.
        • Turn the spoiler setting on to hide story trophy names and descriptions until you earn them.
        • Export your progress before clearing browser data — import brings it back.
